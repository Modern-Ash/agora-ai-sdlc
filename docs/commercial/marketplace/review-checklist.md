# Pre-submission editorial and legal review

**Submission status: BLOCKED**

All checks are intentionally incomplete in the repository. Accountable humans must perform them against the exact portal fields and private-offer documents. Completion belongs in the controlled submission record, not as an unevidenced edit to this template.

## Marketplace operations

- [ ] Seller registration, tax, banking, permissions, and Professional Services eligibility are confirmed.
- [ ] The title is unique and the seller-assigned SKU is approved.
- [ ] At least one directly related AWS service or public AWS Marketplace product is identified and validated.
- [ ] Product categories, keywords, dimensions, visibility, and current portal limits are revalidated.
- [ ] Approved logo and one to three stable public resource URLs are available.
- [ ] Public support contact and operational owners can meet the proposed response and kickoff commitments.
- [ ] Customer-incurred AWS infrastructure charges are disclosed where applicable.

## Editorial and claim review

- [ ] Title, short description, long description, highlights, dimensions, delivery, support, and resources are consistent.
- [ ] Every material assertion maps to exactly one entry in the claim substantiation register.
- [ ] Evidence reflects the release being offered and no roadmap item appears as available.
- [ ] Copy separates open-source software, implementation services, engagement support, and excluded managed operations.
- [ ] AWS is optional deployment context; copy implies no sponsorship, endorsement, certification, or mandatory runtime dependency.
- [ ] Goals, durations, examples, and outcomes are bounded and are not guarantees or unsupported benchmarks.

## Legal, privacy, and security review

- [ ] Counsel approves trademarks, license statements, public terms, offer terms, attachments, and disclaimers.
- [ ] Privacy/security owners approve data handling, access, subprocessors, residency, retention, and incident responsibilities.
- [ ] No customer names, buyer account identifiers, personal contacts, prices, payment schedules, credentials, or negotiated terms appear in the public repository.
- [ ] The exact statement of work defines scope, acceptance, change control, support, exclusions, and responsibility owners.
- [ ] Additional AWS resources, IAM access, agents, or infrastructure needed by the engagement are documented securely when applicable.

## Private-offer review

- [ ] Authorized buyer accounts, duration, currency, pricing, payment schedule, legal terms, and attachments are verified in controlled systems.
- [ ] The offer uses only approved service dimensions and all billed dimensions correspond to actual services.
- [ ] Pre-purchase, post-acceptance, delivery, support, refund, escalation, and completion contacts are assigned.
- [ ] Final portal preview and merged offer documents are reviewed by commercial and legal owners before release.

## Approval record

Store reviewer identities, dates, seller request/offer identifiers, approvals, exceptions, and evidence in the authorized business system. Do not store customer-specific approval records in this public repository.

Only the accountable marketplace owner may change the submission record to approved after editorial, legal, security, and operations reviewers have completed their checks. Issue #43 and this checklist do not authorize submission.
