# AI-DLC Guided Adoption

A bounded Professional Services engagement for teams that want to practice the published AI-DLC method
through Agora Flow instead of adopting it as a prompt library.

This package is an implementation/adoption service, not an AWS service, certification, affiliation or
guarantee of delivery outcome.

## Primary outcome

One delivery team practices a representative Intent through the continuous Agora Flow wizard and leaves
with a configured, provider-neutral AI-DLC operating model that can be repeated on additional Work.

The engagement uses Method Pack 0.2.0 and the documented
[AI-DLC compatibility contract](../method/ai-dlc-compatibility.md).

## What is practiced

The engagement uses a real customer Work wherever practical:

1. normalize the source into an Intent;
2. run Inception through clarification, Level 1 Plan, Stories/criteria, NFR/Risk, Measurement Criteria,
   Units and suggested Bolts;
3. validate human checkpoints and traceability;
4. execute a bounded Construction slice through Domain Design, Logical Design, implementation/tests and
   Deployment Unit where scope permits;
5. review Operations readiness and the authority boundary;
6. inspect the durable artifacts/evidence and replay/resume the wizard.

Brownfield work adds static/dynamic semantic elevation before the Construction slice.

## Adoption experience

Participants do not need to author methodology prompts. Agora Flow exposes:

- current AI-DLC phase and method substep;
- inline explanation of the method concept;
- one bounded clarification question at a time;
- observed method outputs and missing obligations;
- the exact next proposed action through a transparent Decision Card;
- executor/model/decision provenance;
- human authority boundaries and the next validation checkpoint;
- Context Economy measurements from the built-in local Laya decision layer.

The working controls remain the same throughout the engagement:

```text
[Enter] Confirm
[A] Adjust
[D] Details
[X] Exit
```

## Deliverables

- AI-DLC / Agora compatibility baseline for the selected repository/workflow;
- configured Agora AI-SDLC project and adoption profile;
- one traced Intent and its generated/validated method artifacts;
- governance/authority map for Product Owner, Developer and any optional enterprise roles;
- runtime/provider configuration and replacement points;
- Context Economy baseline when enabled: candidate context, selected context, estimated token reduction
  and escalation rate;
- adoption runbook for starting/resuming future Work;
- gap register for customer-specific process, risk, compliance and integrations;
- handover session using the same wizard the team will keep using.

## Boundaries

The package does not promise a fixed productivity percentage, token-saving percentage, cloud bill
reduction, regulatory compliance or autonomous software delivery.

Context/token results are measured against the selected pilot workload and hardware/runtime
configuration. AI-generated outputs remain subject to the human validation boundaries of the method.

## Why Agora Flow

The service is designed to leave behind an executable practice rather than a consultancy-only ritual:

- AI-DLC semantics are captured in Method Pack 0.2.0 and artifact contracts;
- the wizard initiates and guides the conversation;
- Core persists authority, approvals, evidence and lifecycle state;
- artifacts form linked context memory;
- Progressive Intelligence avoids expensive reasoning where simpler intelligence is sufficient;
- the method remains independent of a specific LLM, IDE and cloud provider.

Software remains Apache-2.0; consulting labor and customer-specific integration are commercial services.
